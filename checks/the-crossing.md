# Check: The Crossing (chapter 02) phase gates

Written procedure. Each milestone's done check is the phase gate or gates it covers, from
`docs/build-plan-v1.1.md` section 2. Run by the reviewer and at milestone close; record
pass or fail with evidence in LOG.md.

## P1 (Milestone 1) — Spine
Pass when: every beat cites a TC id or is marked imagined; both standing disagreements (route TC-09a/b, blocking rock TC-15a/b) appear as scenes, not footnotes. Also confirm spec criteria 6 to 10: each beat states the question its view answers; every scale transition is flagged and names the continuity triple and the consequence visible on return to campaign scale; the opening three beats follow orientation, one concrete situation, one interaction, with quiet beats marked; the two candidate beats (Rhone raft construction, roadside column passage) are placed or declined with a one-line reason each; Astra's independent review of the beat sheet is recorded in the spec Review block before the gate is called passed. Evidence: the beat sheet in `data/content/02/`.

## P2 (Milestone 1) — Draft
Pass when: preflight items 1 to 3 pass on the draft prose (every attested claim resolves to an anchor, every inference names its basis, every imagined element is labelled and claim-free). The prose-routing question is resolved before P2 completes.

## P2a (Milestone 1) — Reading test
Pass when: preflight items 1 to 3 pass on the first three beats; the page reads on a real phone without the narrative stopping; the treatment recommendation is written down and filed as a Decision Log row. The three beats are set on one static page at phone width in at least three treatments (per-clause chips A / I / Im; one chip per passage with tap-through to the claims; plain-language labels Ancient account / Reconstruction / Imagined scene), with the same claims and anchors in every treatment and only the display changing. P2 does not continue past three beats until this gate passes.

## P3 (Milestone 2) — Scenes
Pass when: every world state cites its attestation, and no scene visits a place the sources do not put the army (conventions section 7).

## P4 (Milestone 2) — Modules
Pass when: the map-journey module renders the chapter's scene list end to end from committed data, with no hand-placed geography.

## P5 (Milestone 3) — Assemble
Pass when: the chapter reads start to finish on a phone with no dead end.

## P6 (Milestone 3) — Preflight
Pass when: all six conventions section 9 items pass and the result is logged in the Notion Source Log.

## P7 (Milestone 4, MVP) — Ship
Pass when: the chapter is deployed on a URL and read by five people who are not Tony, with their reactions written down.
