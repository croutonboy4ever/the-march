The March

Data spike: real topography for the Rhone-to-Alps corridor (4.0–8.0 E, 43.5–46.0 N)

This page is a proof of concept, not a chapter. It exists to show that the corridor's real terrain, ancient places, roads, and shoreline can be rendered from public source data end to end. The interactive experience, provenance markers, and chapter content come later and are governed by the conventions document in docs/.
